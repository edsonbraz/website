How to Play

Swipe to launch a numbered ball across the circular arena. Control the direction and force with your finger — a quick flick sends it flying, a gentle swipe rolls it into position. Your swipe force directly controls the speed.

When two balls with the same value collide, they merge into one with double the value. 2 becomes 4, 4 becomes 8, 8 becomes 16 — keep merging all the way up to 2048 and beyond.

Balls bounce off the arena walls and each other with realistic physics. Plan your shots carefully — the arena fills up fast. When 12 balls fill the ring with no merges possible, it's game over.

1. Introduction

Edson Braz ("Developer", "we", "our") operates Merge Ring. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use our app.

2. Information Stored Locally

All game data is stored on your device only:

  • Game state and progress (current score, ball positions, next ball value)
  • Game records and player statistics (high scores, games played, highest merge)
  • Settings preferences (haptics, screen shake, swipe sensitivity, selected theme)
  • In-app purchase status (Remove Ads, Pro Themes)
  • Saved game state for resume functionality

We do not create user accounts, and we do not collect personal information.

Users who purchase Remove Ads will never see advertisements, and AdMob is completely deactivated for those users.

See Google's Privacy Policy.

Merge Ring requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) consent before initializing the AdMob SDK. If you decline tracking, ads will still appear but will not be personalized. Purchasing Remove Ads eliminates all advertising and related data collection entirely.

5. How Information Is Used

Local data is used solely to save your game progress, statistics, and preferences. Advertising data is used by Google to serve ads. We do not profile users, engage in cross-app tracking, or use data for marketing purposes.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your data. The only third party that receives data is Google, through the AdMob SDK, and only for users who have not purchased Remove Ads. Users who purchase Remove Ads have zero third-party data sharing.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All game data is stored locally on your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app. Any data collected by third-party services (Google AdMob) is subject to their respective retention policies. We do not maintain any server-side databases.

9. Your Rights
