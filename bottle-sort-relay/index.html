How to Play

Each bottle holds up to four layers of colored liquid. Tap a bottle to pick it up, then tap another to pour. Colors flow only when the top colors match — or the destination is empty.

Your goal: sort every bottle so each one contains a single color. As levels progress, you'll face more colors, tighter timers, and locked bottles that only unlock when you sort a neighbor.

Stuck? Use Hints to reveal the next optimal move, or Undo to reverse mistakes. Need to earn stars for speed and efficiency across 10 chapters of escalating difficulty.

Two Ways to Play

⏱

Timed Mode

The pressure is on. Every second you save carries over to the next level — bank time for the harder chapters ahead. Stars are awarded based on how much time remains when you finish.

☕

Relaxed Mode

No timer. Take your time and focus on finding the most efficient solution. Stars are awarded based on your move count versus the optimal par. Includes a step-by-step solution reveal for Pro users.

Privacy Policy

Effective: February 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Edson Braz ("Developer", "we", "our") operates Bottle Sort Relay. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use our app.

2. Information Stored Locally

All game data is stored on your device only:

  • Level progress and completion status
  • Stars earned and best times/moves
  • Tutorial completion state
  • Settings preferences (audio, haptics, theme)
  • Pro upgrade purchase status (stored securely in Keychain)
  • Review prompt state

We do not create user accounts, and we do not collect personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements only. There are exactly three user-initiated ad placements: earning extra hints, earning extra undos, and earning bonus time. We do not display interstitial or banner advertisements.

When you watch a rewarded ad, Google may collect: IP address, device identifiers, ad interaction data, and performance/crash diagnostics.

Pro users never see advertisements, and AdMob is completely inactive for Pro users.

See Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple StoreKit

All in-app purchase processing is handled by Apple through StoreKit. The Developer does not receive, store, or process any payment information.

Apple Game Center

Game Center integration exists in the app but is currently disabled. This Privacy Policy will be updated before any Game Center features are enabled.

4. How Information Is Used

Local data is used solely to save your game progress and preferences. Advertising data is used by Google to serve relevant rewarded ads. We do not profile users, engage in cross-app tracking, or use data for marketing purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your data. The only third party that receives data is Google, through the AdMob SDK, and only for free (non-Pro) users who choose to watch rewarded ads. Pro users have zero third-party data sharing.

6. Children's Privacy

Bottle Sort Relay is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from any user, including children. No registration or account creation is required to play. Parents and guardians can eliminate all advertisements and third-party data collection by purchasing the Pro upgrade.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All game data is stored locally on your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app. Any data collected by third-party services (Google AdMob) is subject to their respective retention policies. We do not maintain any server-side databases.

8. Your Rights

Because no data is stored on our servers, there is no server-side data to request or delete. To remove all local game data, simply uninstall the app. To opt out of personalized advertising, adjust your settings in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. To eliminate all third-party data collection entirely, purchase the Pro upgrade.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
