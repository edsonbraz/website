1. Introduction

Edson Braz ("Developer", "we", "our") develops and publishes mobile games for iOS. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled across all of our apps, including Bottle Sort Relay, Fall Catch, Gap Runner, and Merge Ring.

Our games fall into two categories:

  • Ad-supported games — Bottle Sort Relay, Fall Catch, Merge Ring (free to play with optional in-app purchases to remove ads)
  • Ad-free games — Gap Runner (no advertisements)

The sections below explain what applies to each type. Where a section only applies to certain games, it is clearly marked.

2. What We Don't Collect

We want to be upfront: we do not collect, store, or process any personal information on our servers. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or physical addresses
  • Phone numbers or contact lists
  • Photos, camera data, or microphone input
  • Location data (GPS or otherwise)
  • Browsing history or search data
  • Health, fitness, or biometric data

We do not create user accounts, and no registration is required to play any of our games. We do not operate any servers or databases that store user data.

3. Information Stored Locally

All game data is stored exclusively on your device. This includes:

  • Game state and progress (scores, levels, positions)
  • Player statistics (high scores, games played, achievements)
  • Settings preferences (sound, haptics, visual options)
  • In-app purchase status (where applicable)
  • Saved game state for resume functionality

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. It is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Bottle Sort Relay Fall Catch Merge Ring

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. The ad format varies by game:

  • Bottle Sort Relay — rewarded ads only (user-initiated: extra hints, extra undos, bonus time). No banners or interstitials.
  • Fall Catch — banner ads on menus and game-over screens, plus an optional rewarded video to continue after game over.
  • Merge Ring — banner advertisements and interstitial ads between games. No rewarded ads.

When ads are displayed, Google may collect: IP address, device identifiers, ad interaction data, and performance/crash diagnostics.

Users who purchase the ad-removal option (Pro upgrade in Bottle Sort Relay, Remove Ads in Fall Catch and Merge Ring) will never see advertisements, and AdMob is completely deactivated for those users — eliminating all ad-related data collection.

See Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple StoreKit

Bottle Sort Relay Fall Catch Merge Ring

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. The Developer does not receive, store, or process any payment or billing information.

Apple Game Center

Fall Catch Gap Runner

Fall Catch and Gap Runner integrate with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. When you sign in to Game Center, Apple may process your player display name and gameplay data. Fall Catch submits three leaderboards (one per difficulty mode). Gap Runner submits two leaderboards (high score, highest zone) and 10 achievements (zone milestones, score milestones, power-up collection, close calls).

Game Center is managed entirely by Apple, and any data associated with your Game Center profile is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. App Tracking Transparency

Fall Catch Merge Ring

Fall Catch and Merge Ring request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) consent before initializing the AdMob SDK, as required by Apple. If you decline tracking, ads will still appear but will not be personalized. Purchasing Remove Ads eliminates all advertising and related data collection entirely.

Bottle Sort Relay uses only user-initiated rewarded ads and does not request ATT consent. Gap Runner does not include any advertising SDK and does not request ATT consent.

6. How Information Is Used

Local data is used solely to save your game progress, statistics, and preferences. Advertising data (in ad-supported games) is used by Google to serve ads. We do not profile users, engage in cross-app tracking, or use data for marketing purposes.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your data to anyone. Third-party data sharing is limited to:

  • Google — through the AdMob SDK in our ad-supported games, only for users who have not purchased the ad-removal option
  • Apple — through Game Center in Fall Catch and Gap Runner, for leaderboard and achievement functionality

Users who purchase the ad-removal option (Pro upgrade or Remove Ads) eliminate all ad-related third-party data sharing.

8. Children's Privacy

All of our games are rated 4+ and are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including children. No registration or account creation is required to play.

Parents and guardians can eliminate all advertisements and ad-related data collection in our ad-supported games by purchasing the ad-removal option (Pro upgrade in Bottle Sort Relay, Remove Ads in Fall Catch and Merge Ring).

9. Data Retention and Deletion

All game data is stored locally on your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app. We do not maintain any server-side databases or backups of your data.

Any data collected by third-party services (Google AdMob, Apple Game Center) is subject to their respective retention policies.

10. Your Rights

Because we do not store any data on our servers, there is no server-side data to request, access, or delete. You are always in full control of your data:

  • Delete all local data — uninstall the app from your device
  • Opt out of personalized ads — go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Eliminate all ad-related data collection — purchase the ad-removal option (Pro upgrade in Bottle Sort Relay, Remove Ads in Fall Catch and Merge Ring)
  • Manage Game Center data — adjust in iOS Settings > Game Center

If you are located in the EU, UK, California, Brazil, or another jurisdiction with specific privacy rights and have questions about exercising those rights, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
