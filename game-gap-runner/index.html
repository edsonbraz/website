How to Play

Hold to shrink your glowing ball to squeeze through narrow gaps between oncoming wall segments. Release to grow back to full size. The tighter the gap, the smaller you need to be — and the more precise your timing.

Manage your energy carefully — shrinking drains it fast. Collect golden orbs to recharge. Your energy bar shifts from green to red as it depletes. Hit zero and you can't shrink anymore — leaving you vulnerable.

Survive through 100 progressive zones where walls accelerate and gaps tighten. Grab power-ups — shield, slow-mo, double points, and ghost — to push further. Milestone celebrations at zones 25, 50, 75, and 100.

Privacy Policy

Effective: February 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Edson Braz ("Developer", "we", "our") operates Gap Runner. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use our app.

2. Information Stored Locally

All game data is stored on your device only:

  • High scores and zone progression records
  • Settings preferences (sound, music, haptics)
  • Close call counts (used for achievement tracking)

We do not create user accounts, and we do not collect personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

Apple Game Center

Gap Runner integrates with Apple Game Center to provide leaderboards and achievements. When you sign in to Game Center, Apple may process your player display name and gameplay data (high score, highest zone reached, achievement progress).

We submit two leaderboards (high score, highest zone) and 10 achievements (zone milestones, score milestones, power-up collection, close calls). This data is processed by Apple according to their privacy practices.

See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. App Tracking Transparency

Gap Runner does not track users across other apps or websites. We do not use any advertising SDKs and do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) consent because there is no tracking to consent to.

5. How Information Is Used

Local data is used solely to save your game progress and preferences. Game Center data is used to display leaderboards and track achievement progress. We do not profile users, engage in cross-app tracking, or use data for marketing purposes.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your data. The only third party that receives gameplay data is Apple, through Game Center, for leaderboard and achievement functionality.

7. Children's Privacy

Gap Runner is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from any user, including children. No registration or account creation is required to play.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All game data is stored locally on your device and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app. Game Center data (leaderboard scores and achievements) is managed by Apple and subject to their retention policies. We do not maintain any server-side databases.

9. Your Rights

Because no data is stored on our servers, there is no server-side data to request or delete. To remove all local game data, simply uninstall the app. To manage Game Center data, visit your Apple ID settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
