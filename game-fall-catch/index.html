How to Play

A glowing ball falls through a neon grid. Tap anywhere to create a temporary platform beneath it. Time your taps — platforms fade after a few seconds and you have a limited supply.

Collect coins as they appear for bonus points. The ball bounces off platforms and screen edges — use the physics to your advantage and aim for coin pickups between bounces.

Watch the danger zone at the bottom. If the ball falls past your last platform, you lose a life. On Hard mode, you only get one. Choose from Easy, Normal, or Hard — each with its own Game Center leaderboard.

What Makes It Click

🔴

One-Tap Gameplay

Tap anywhere to place a platform. That's it. Simple to learn, brutally hard to master. Every session starts in seconds — perfect for quick bursts or marathon runs.

🏆

3 Difficulty Modes

Easy gives you 3 lives and wide platforms. Normal ramps up gravity with each bounce. Hard gives you one life, tiny shrinking platforms, and punishing tap cooldowns. Each mode has its own leaderboard.

🎨

5 Neon Themes

Play in Neon, Midnight Ocean, Sunset Blaze, Toxic Matrix, or Arctic Frost. Each theme transforms the entire visual experience with unique color palettes and glow effects.

💫

Feel Every Bounce

Haptic feedback pulses with every bounce and coin pickup. Neon particle effects trail behind the ball. Sound and music react to your gameplay. All toggleable in settings.
